# Release Notes

User-facing release notes as shown by Sous in Telegram. For technical changes, see [CHANGELOG.md](./CHANGELOG.md).

## v1.5.0

A bunch of under-the-hood upgrades just landed. Things should feel noticeably snappier:

- **Streaming replies** -- My responses now appear word-by-word instead of making you wait for the whole thing
- **Dark mode** -- Open the Mini App settings to switch between light and dark themes, plus adjust font size
- **Smarter reminders** -- Start-cooking alerts now factor in actual recipe prep and cook times
- **Tougher behind the scenes** -- Better handling of busy periods, safer data handling, and cost guardrails so things stay reliable

Open the Mini App and check out Settings to try the new theme options!

## v1.4.0

Hey! I've been busy learning new tricks since we last caught up. Here's the highlight reel:

- **Recipe links** -- Drop a recipe URL into our chat and I'll pull out the recipe for you
- **Recipe photos** -- Snap a pic of a cookbook page or handwritten recipe and I'll read it
- **Smarter saving** -- I check for duplicates now, and I'll notice recipes in conversation and offer to save them
- **Store-aware groceries** -- Your grocery lists now split items by your store preferences
- **Recipe tweaks** -- Ask me to modify a recipe and I'll update it in place instead of creating a new one

Try sending me a recipe link or photo!

P.S. Your feedback helps me get better -- just type /feedback anytime to share what's working and what's not.
