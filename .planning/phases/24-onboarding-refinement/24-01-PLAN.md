  <action>
Rewrite the `buildRecipesPrompt()` function in `src/onboarding/prompt.ts`. Replace the current passive prompt with a directive one that:

1. **Opens with a specific, concrete question** -- not "tell me about meals you make" but something like "What are 3-5 meals your household makes on regular rotation? Think about what you cooked last week, or your go-to weeknight dinners."

2. **Explains WHY** -- Frame it as: the more recipes Claude knows, the better the first meal plan will be. "I'll use these to put together your first weekly dinner plan."

3. **Sets a target** -- Mention "3-5 of your regular go-to meals" as a goal. This is a soft target, not a hard gate.

4. **Encourages continued sharing** -- After saving each recipe, prompt for more: "What else is in your regular rotation?" or "Any other go-to meals?"

5. **Gentle nudge for low count** -- If user wants to stop with fewer than 3, gently encourage: "Even one or two more would help me put together a better first meal plan for you."

6. **Offer first meal plan on completion** -- When the recipes phase wraps up, offer: "Now that I know some of your go-to meals, want me to put together a dinner plan for this week?" If user says yes, complete onboarding and let the normal meal plan flow handle it. If user declines, complete onboarding normally.

7. **Preserve existing patterns** -- Keep the same structure: `<onboarding>` XML wrapper, `${SKIP_HANDLING}` interpolation, `${SHARED_RULES}` interpolation, `__ONBOARDING_PHASE_COMPLETE:recipes__` marker at the end.

8. **Do NOT change** -- The state machine in `state.ts`, the flow ordering (preferences -> tour -> recipes -> complete), the other prompt functions (preferences, tour, tour_only), or the SHARED_RULES/SKIP_HANDLING constants.

The key insight: Claude already knows HOW to detect and save recipes from conversation (Phase 21 implicit detection). This prompt just needs to be more directive about ASKING for them and explaining why they matter.
